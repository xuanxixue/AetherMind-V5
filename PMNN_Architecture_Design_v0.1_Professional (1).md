## 0. Executive Summary

This document specifies the Physical Matrix Neural Network (PMNN), a universal foundational architecture that supersedes the Transformer paradigm. PMNN replaces the token-attention paradigm — predicated on statistical affinity — with a complex-valued oscillator field governed by phase synchronization, a physical coupling mechanism. The depth dimension is reformulated as a physical time ordinary differential equation (ODE), and the output layer is restructured to produce ELF-style continuous denoising trajectories.

The resulting architecture is modality-agnostic and occupies an isomorphic structural position relative to the Transformer. Any input modality is first subjected to "oscillatorization" to enter a continuous embedding field, evolves through *R* rounds of physical blocks, and is discretized back into symbolic representation at the terminal step.

## 2. Unified State Representation: Oscillator Factor as Complex-Valued Field Element

```
z_i = a_i ⊙ e^(iθ_i) ∈ ℂ^d   |   Z = (A, Θ) ∈ ℝ^(N×d) × ℝ^(N×d)
```

- **a_i ∈ ℝ^d_+**: Amplitude vector (encoding energy/confidence; preserves individual oscillator information — a critical channel for preventing full-synchronization collapse);
- **θ_i ∈ ℝ^d**: Phase vector (the carrier of information integration, evolved through coupling dynamics);
- **N**: Oscillator count, equivalent to sequence length (token/patch/frame);
- **d**: Channel dimension (field depth).

**Complexification** (mapping from arbitrary modality embedding e_i): `a_i = softplus(W_a e_i + b_a)`, `θ_i = W_θ e_i mod 2π`. This formulation is modality-agnostic: text tokens, image patches, spatiotemporal video patches, and audio frames uniformly enter through the same gateway.

## 3. Physical Block: The Universal Layer Paralleling the Transformer Block

A stack of *R* physical blocks is structurally homologous to Transformer layer stacking. Each block comprises four sublayers:

### 3.1 Phase Synchronization Layer (Kuramoto Coupling — Replacing Attention) — Extension 3

```
dθ_i^ν / dt = ω_i^ν + Σ_j W_ij · sin(θ_j^ν − θ_i^ν) + κ(t)·g_i^ν
```

- **ω_i^ν**: Intrinsic frequency (input drive, per oscillator per channel);
- **W ∈ ℝ^(N×N)**: Coupling matrix (learnable; low-rank factorization W = UVᵀ with r = 32, or Toeplitz distance-structured — informed by UN-0's sparsity findings);
- **κ(t)**: Synchronization strength, designated the **K-value** (time field × thermodynamics); governs the chaos-to-synchronization phase transition;
- **g_i**: Drift field guidance term (external force; see §3.3);
- **Order parameter**: r(t) = |(1/N)Σ_j e^(iθ_j)| ∈ [0,1]. The condition r → 1 indicates full synchronization, i.e., completion of information integration. The K-value governs whether each block operates in the exploration regime (r ≈ 0) or the decision regime (r ≈ 1) via the Kuramoto critical coupling threshold κ_c.

**Computational complexity** is of the same order as attention: full coupling O(N²·d); structured W reduces this to O(N·d·r).

### 3.2 Resonance–Standing-Wave Feed-Forward Layer — Extension 5

Each FNN round implements frequency-selective amplification with positional modulation, refining the drift direction:

```
h = σ( W₂·( W₁·z + b₁ ) ⊙ sw(z, ω_d) + b₂ )
```

- **Resonance gain** (Lorentzian frequency selectivity): A(ω_d, ω_n) = 1 / √((ω_d² − ω_n²)² + (2γω_d)²). When the driving frequency ω_d (derived from the K-value) approaches a channel's intrinsic frequency ω_n, that channel undergoes amplification;
- **Standing-wave modulation** (position-aware, with oscillator index serving as spatial coordinate): SW_m(p) = cos(k_m·p), where k_m = 2πm/N. Forward and backward waves superpose to form standing waves; the resulting node/antinode structure serves as a positional basis — a physical analogue of RoPE;
- The FNN output constitutes "the field subsequent to resonance selection," iteratively refining the direction prescribed by the K-value in each round.

### 3.3 Drift Field Layer — Extension 2 (Implemented per Verified Mechanism)

```
V(Z) = V⁺(Z) − λ·V⁻(Z)

V⁺ = Σ_k softmax_τ(−‖z_i − z_k^tar‖²)·(z_k^tar − z_i)   (real-sample attraction)
V⁻ = Σ_j softmax_τ(−‖z_i − z_j‖²)·(z_j − z_i)           (generated-sample mutual repulsion, preventing collapse)
```

- Multi-temperature τ summation provides multi-scale coverage (following the original paper); loss form: L_drift = MSE(φ(x), stopgrad(φ(x)+V));
- **Architectural Departure (as specified)**: In the original formulation, the drift field is active only during training, with a one-step forward pass at inference. In the present architecture, the drift direction v_φ(Z, t) is recomputed at every physical block, transforming "training-time evolution" into "real-time inference evolution." This specification is retained, with the corresponding verification point documented in §11.

### 3.4 Thermodynamics–Entropy Layer — Extension 4

- **Energy (XY Model Hamiltonian)**: U(Z) = −Σ_i,j W_ij·cos(θ_j − θ_i). Key identity: ∂U/∂θ_i = Σ_j W_ij·sin(θ_j − θ_i). The sin coupling of Kuramoto is precisely the gradient of the XY energy, establishing that the phase synchronization layer is fundamentally an energy-descent dynamic (phase locking ⟺ energy minimization);
- **Entropy**: S(Z) = −Σ_i p_i log p_i, where p_i = softmax(a_i) (entropy of the amplitude distribution). The drift field repulsion term V⁻ is equivalent to the entropy pressure −T·∇S, providing a regularization-theoretic justification for collapse prevention;
- **Free Energy**: F = U − T·S. Temperature schedule T(t) implements annealing: T(t) = T_max·(1−t)^p + T_min, cooling as t: 0→1;
- **Noise Injection** (fluctuation-dissipation theorem): η ~ N(0, 2T/Δt), a Langevin term governing exploration magnitude during training and sampling.

### 3.5 Time Physics Layer — Extension 6

- **Depth as Physical Time**: The entire stack constitutes an ODE, dZ/dt = f_φ(Z, t, K(t)), t ∈ [0,1], integrated via Heun or RK4 methods (reversible, rendering memory consumption independent of depth);
- **Time-Reversal Symmetry**: Encoding/understanding corresponds to forward integration (t: 0→1, field synchronization); generation corresponds to time reversal (high-entropy noise state → low-entropy data state, driven by the K-value, with entropy exported through the open system);
- **Time Field**: Provides the global temporal coordinate and the driving protocol curves κ(t), T(t), ω_d(t) (see §7).

## 5. Unified State Equation (Core Matrix Formulation Integrating All Six Extensions)

```
dZ/dt = M_κ(t)·Z + ∇_Z[ −U(Z) + T(t)·S(Z) ] + v_φ(Z, t) + η(t)
```

- **M_κ(t)·Z**: The linear matrix operator navigated by the K-value (time field × thermodynamics → κ(t); subsuming coupling strength scaling, frequency selection matrix, and the linear component of drift). This constitutes the linear principal axis of the matrix formulation;
- **∇_Z[−U + T·S]**: Physical potential energy descent + entropy pressure. Given U = −Σ_ij W_ij·cos(θ_j − θ_i), this term automatically expands to yield the Kuramoto sin coupling (Extension 3), free energy dynamics (Extension 4), and drift repulsion term (Extension 2) — three extensions converge within a single gradient term;
- **v_φ(Z, t)**: Drift field learned residual (Extension 2; active at every round during inference);
- **η(t)**: Langevin noise (temperature injection; fluctuation-dissipation; Extension 4).

> **Transparency Note**: The overall formulation constitutes a hybrid dynamics of "linear operator + nonlinear gradient term + learned residual + noise." It is not a purely linear matrix formulation. The composition follows the verified mechanisms; no claim of full linearity is made.

## 6. ELF Output Fusion (Extension 1)

- **Trajectory**: The physical core does not directly emit terminal oscillator factors. Instead, it outputs a denoising trajectory {x_t} in the continuous embedding space, with interpolation x_t = t·x₁ + (1−t)x₀ and x₀ ~ N(0, I) (rectified flow, consistent with ELF);
- **Conditioning Signal**: The K-value direction (κ(t), ω_d(t), T(t) as provided by the time field × thermodynamics) serves as the flow matching condition *c*. This confers greater precision than conventional conditioning signals through the addition of physical constraints (synchronization degree, temperature, entropy budget);
- **Weight Sharing**: The physical core serves as the velocity field network v_θ(x_t, t, c); denoising and subsequent decoding share weights (ELF-consistent);
- **Oscillator Decoding Network — Dual-Branch Architecture**:
  - **Denoising Branch** (t < t_dec): Continues trajectory refinement / velocity field prediction;
  - **Decoding Branch** (t ≥ t_dec): De-embedding matrix E ∈ ℝ^(d×V) projects continuous embeddings back to discrete tokens;
  - **Mode Gating**: g(t) = σ(W_g·[t, r(t), κ(t)]), with t_dec ≈ 0.98. The proposed "mode token mechanism" is operationalized as a continuous gating scalar;
- **ELF-Consistent Techniques**: Training target embeddings are generated by a frozen T5 encoder (discarded at inference); the trajectory space employs a 128-dimensional bottleneck.

## 7. Training Protocol

1. **Aggregate Loss**: L = L_fm + λ₁L_sync + λ₂L_drift + λ₃L_F
   - L_fm = E‖v_θ(x_t, t, c) − (x₁ − x₀)‖² (flow matching velocity matching; ELF primary loss);
   - L_sync = ‖r(t) − r_target(t)‖² (order parameter tracking the K-curriculum);
   - L_drift = MSE(φ(x), stopgrad(φ(x)+V)) (drift field consistency; original paper loss);
   - L_F = U(Z) − T·S(Z) (free energy regularization; thermodynamics layer).
2. **K-Curriculum**: κ(t) progresses from weak to strong (initial chaotic exploration followed by synchronization and integration), corresponding to the diffusion noise schedule. κ(t) = κ_max·σ((t − t_c)/τ), with the synchronization phase transition occurring at t_c;
3. **Two-Stage Training**:
   - **Stage A**: Pretrain the physical core on frozen encoder targets (synchronization + resonance basis + drift field);
   - **Stage B**: End-to-end flow matching fine-tuning (T annealing + noise injection);
4. **Numerical Methods**: Heun/RK4 integration with reversible layers, ensuring training memory is independent of depth.

## 11. Calibration and Risk Assessment

- **Precise Boundary of "Non-Statistical"**: What is removed is the attention mechanism and token-level stepwise cross-entropy. Flow matching and the drift field remain, in essence, distribution modeling (statistical targets). Precise formulation: **The mechanism is physical; the distributional target is statistical.** The two are not contradictory and require no evasion;
- **Downgrading the "Unprecedented" Claim**: ELF, Drifting, UN-0, and Kuramoto synchronization networks each possess precedent in machine learning. What genuinely lacks precedent is the specific combination of "modality-agnostic physical matrix universal substrate" + K-value navigation + inference-time drift field activity. The combination is novel; the constituent components are not;
- **Full-Synchronization Collapse** (homogenization of individual information as r → 1): Mitigation = heterogeneous intrinsic frequencies ω_i + amplitude channel preserving individual information + sparse/local coupling;
- **Continuous-Space Training Instability** (documented ELF limitation: substantial divergence between embedding distribution and Gaussian noise): Mitigation = 128-dim bottleneck + normalization + K-curriculum;
- **ODE Numerical Error Accumulation**: Mitigated via Heun/RK4 + residual connections;
- **Inference-Time Drift Field Activity**: Constitutes an intentional departure from the original paper. Validation is required for both the stability and additional computational cost (×R) of per-round one-step distribution alignment;
- **Licensing**: UN-0 weights are released under CC-BY-NC-4.0 (non-commercial). Commercial deployment requires independent implementation of the Kuramoto layer. All mathematical formulations in this design are public and present no implementation barrier;
- **Boundary of "Physics"**: Thermodynamics, resonance, and time physics enter as computable, differentiable, and measurable mathematical operators (XY energy, Lorentzian resonance, ODE time reversal). These are analogous operators, not literal physical claims.
